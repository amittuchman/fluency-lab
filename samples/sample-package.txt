// ---- Vocabulary ----
@vocab reluctant | unwilling and hesitant to do something | He was reluctant to commit to a deadline. | he: לא להוט, מהסס | pos: adj | #adjectives
@vocab trade-off | a balance between two things you cannot fully have at the same time | There's always a trade-off between speed and accuracy. | he: פשרה, ויתור על דבר אחד לטובת אחר | pos: noun | #economics
@vocab clear-cut | easy to understand, with no room for doubt | The evidence isn't as clear-cut as it seems. | he: חד-משמעי | pos: adj | #natural-phrasing
@vocab leverage | to use something you have in order to get a better result | We can leverage our data to make better decisions. | he: למנף | pos: verb | #business
@vocab bottleneck | the point where a process slows down because of a limit | Hiring has become the main bottleneck for the team. | he: צוואר בקבוק | pos: noun | #business
@vocab nuanced | showing small but important differences | She gave a nuanced answer to a difficult question. | he: רגיש לדקויות, מורכב | pos: adj | #adjectives
@vocab rule of thumb | a practical rule based on experience rather than exact theory | As a rule of thumb, save at least ten percent of your income. | he: כלל אצבע | pos: noun phrase | #idioms
@vocab follow through | to finish what you started or promised to do | He has great ideas, but he rarely follows through. | he: לבצע עד הסוף | pos: phrasal verb | coll: follow through on a promise; follow through with a plan | #phrasal-verbs
@vocab incentive | something that encourages a person or firm to do something | Tax cuts give firms an incentive to invest. | he: תמריץ | pos: noun | #economics

// ---- Key sentences ----
@sentence The trade-off isn't as clear-cut as it looks. | he: הפשרה כאן פחות חד-משמעית ממה שהיא נראית. | #natural-phrasing
@sentence That's a fair point, but I see it a bit differently. | he: זו נקודה הוגנת, אבל אני רואה את זה קצת אחרת. | #disagreeing
@sentence Let me think out loud for a second. | he: תן לי לחשוב בקול לרגע. | #conversation
@sentence It depends on how you define success. | he: זה תלוי איך מגדירים הצלחה. | #conversation

// ---- Recurring mistakes ----
@fix I very agree with you. => I completely agree with you. | rule: "Very" can't modify a verb. Use "completely", "totally" or "really". | #adverbs
@fix He explained me the problem. => He explained the problem to me. | rule: "Explain" needs "to" before the person: explain something to someone. | #verb-patterns
@fix I am working here since 2020. => I have been working here since 2020. | rule: With "since" or "for" and a period that continues until now, use the present perfect (continuous), not the present continuous. | #tenses
@fix It depends of the situation. => It depends on the situation. | rule: "Depend" takes "on". | #prepositions
@fix I didn't went to the meeting. => I didn't go to the meeting. | rule: After "did" or "didn't", use the base form of the verb. | #tenses

// ---- More natural phrasing ----
@natural I think this solution is very good. => I think this approach makes a lot of sense. | #natural-phrasing
@natural I want to say something about this. => Can I jump in here for a second? | note: A natural way to take a turn in a discussion. | #conversation
@natural This is a very big problem. => This is a serious problem. | note: "Serious" is the usual collocation with "problem". | #collocations
@natural We need to make the decision fast. => We need to make a quick decision. | #collocations
